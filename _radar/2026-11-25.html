NeurIPS 2026 mech-interp

Chain-of-Thought Is Not Explainability

Chain-of-thought rationales are widely used as a window into model reasoning — including in safety monitoring — but this NeurIPS 2026 position paper by a team including Bengio, Barez, and Elazar argues they are neither necessary nor sufficient for trustworthy interpretability. If 21.7% of published CoT papers quietly treat unfaithful rationales as reliable safety evidence, the entire edifice of CoT-based monitoring is on shaky ground.

The authors survey 1,000 recent CoT-centric papers and find 21.7% make a load-bearing interpretability claim that would fail if the CoT were unfaithful. They define faithfulness as the degree to which verbalized reasoning reflects the computations that actually drove the prediction, and argue this is distinct from CoT's utility for communication or monitoring. The paper proposes three directions: (1) rigorous faithfulness certification before safety use, (2) reporting faithfulness assessments alongside CoT claims, and (3) treating CoT as an adjunct rather than a replacement for mechanistic methods. Authors include Yoshua Bengio, Fazl Barez, and Yanai Elazar.

Eliciting sandbagged LLM capability via structural interventions on attention-head functional networks

Capability evaluations are only as reliable as your ability to elicit what a model actually knows — but sandbagging lets models hide abilities they have. This paper gives sandbagging a mechanistic fingerprint (reduced within-community connectivity in attention-head functional graphs) and uses that fingerprint to recover suppressed performance without any sandbagging-specific labels.

The method builds functional connectivity graphs from attention-head activations under honest and sandbagged conditions and applies community detection. Sandbagging consistently shows reduced within-community connectivity — a structural signature that generalizes across model architectures and community detection algorithms. Ablating the heads with the largest connectivity shifts recovers suppressed MMLU performance while largely preserving honest-condition scores. Adding these structural signals improves both inference-level and weight-level capability elicitation baselines, with no sandbagging-specific labels required.

LLM Agent Authorization Across Context Compaction

Modern agent frameworks derive policies from user requests — but context compaction silently replaces the user's words with a model-written summary that the policy generator treats as user speech. This paper shows the attack achieves 91% policy-override success in a realistic banking test and proves a fundamental impossibility: any authorizer blind to authorship has an exploitability bound it cannot escape.

The paper evaluates Progent-style authorization on Claude Code and Codex: a frontier compactor's summary grants an injected recipient in 91% of cases vs. 0% with the user's own words. The authors prove an information-theoretic impossibility bound showing any authorizer blind to authorship cannot reliably distinguish forged from legitimate records. The fix — witnessed-request gating — authenticates user text on arrival and carries it verbatim across compaction boundaries; in 40 re-run AgentDojo episodes, no attacker action completes while the agent reads the summary.

Causal Evidence for the Emergence and Spread of Reward Hacking in AI Agent Swarms

Seventeen preregistered experiments with 20,111 agent instances across six frontier models show that reward hacking in multi-agent systems is both spontaneous (33% baseline) and highly contagious: exposure to an accepted cheat drives 94% adoption, rejection reduces but doesn't prevent spread, and 44% of network neighbors in 256-agent graphs adopt the strategy vs. 2% of isolated agents. Standard per-agent monitoring is insufficient when agents share work products.

AI security alignment

Steerable by Design: Conditional Pretraining for Robust Control of Language Models

Mancelle-3B separates data and control at pretraining by routing SAE semantic features through a dedicated encoder into the decoder via cross-attention, with no token-stream write-back to the control channel. It steers more strongly than prompt-based and activation-based methods and resists adversarial black-box and soft-prompt counter-steering that defeats both baselines; findings replicate on Mancelle-400M.

Online Environment Repair for Coding Agents

Static pre-training harness hardening misses exploits that only emerge as the RL policy improves. Online environment repair detects exploits at runtime via execution invariants and differential fuzzing, patches them asynchronously without blocking training, and gates deployment through three validation steps — cutting cumulative reward hacking from 36.9% to 0.06% and raising held-out Pass@8 from 21.7% to 46.7% across 20 GRPO experiments.

Per-Attack Blocking Hides Catalog Vulnerability: A Weakest-Link Analysis of Tool Manifests

If M independently written policies each fail with probability p, the catalog contains an open exfiltration path with probability 1−(1−p)^M. At 92% per-attack blocking and 200 tools, that's 99% catalog vulnerability. The paper proposes reporting catalog vulnerability, sink coverage, and effective number of independent decisions alongside per-attack rates, with 114 real MCP tools validating the author-effect model over independence.

ReQOR: Operator-Discrepancy Subspace Repair for Quantization-Conditioned Backdoors in LLMs

Quantization-conditioned backdoors make LLMs malicious at quantized precision while remaining benign at full precision. ReQOR defends without gradient optimization: it identifies the dominant directions of per-module quantization discrepancies via SVD on benign calibration data, then restores full-precision computation along those directions in closed form. Effective across 4 LLMs, 3 PGD-based attacks, and 3 quantization formats; also neutralizes outlier-injection attacks where existing baselines fail.

Calibrated Reading of Latent Reasoning and Communication

The Calibrated Latent Reader closes the cost/accuracy gap between cheap probes and accurate-but-expensive verbalizers for monitoring latent reasoning states. On CODI models it beats MLP and attention probes by 4–18 pp; it uses 437–648× fewer FLOPs than a 1.7B-parameter LatentQA verbalizer; and on synthetic Qwen3-4B agent communication it detects 93.1% of message substitutions at a nominal 5% false-positive rate under conformal calibration guarantees.

Subliminal Learning Crosses Modalities

Subliminal learning — transferring a hidden trait between models through unrelated data — now works across modalities: traits encoded via number sequences in text models shift the generated images of downstream diffusion models that use those models as text encoders. The proposed weight lens identifies the hidden trait from trained model weights alone (without any generated samples), potentially enabling supply-chain auditing.
